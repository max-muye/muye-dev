FIELD GUIDE

Your first day

  1. Mine a tree to collect logs.
  2. Craft each log into four wooden planks.
  3. Mine dirt and stone, then build a shelter.
  4. Craft tools, mine coal and iron, then smelt iron in a furnace.
  5. Collect drops, plant seeds, eat food, and use torches to survive the night.

The world autosaves after changes and when you leave the page.
