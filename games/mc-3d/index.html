How to play
  • WASD or the touch pad moves. Drag or move the mouse to look.
  • Click or tap Mine to break the block in the crosshair.
  • Right-click or tap Place to place your selected block.
  • Use Place on a lever to switch it. Dust carries power, gates point forward, and powered TNT explodes.
  • Worlds save automatically in your browser.
