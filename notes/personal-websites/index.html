Personal websites

A domain can be a workshop.

July 29, 2026

A personal site does not need to arrive fully formed. It can be a place to test a thought, publish a small tool, or leave a visible trail of what is changing.

Make it useful first

The strongest personal websites have a point of view and a few things worth returning to. A short note, a working experiment, or a careful explanation can do more than a polished list of claims.

Over time, the workshop becomes a record. That record is often more convincing than a portfolio assembled all at once.
